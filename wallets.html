Keeping your Bitcoin safe

A short, honest introduction. No wallet is risk-free, and nothing here is advice.

Exchange vs self-custody

On an exchange (like Revolut X), the company holds your Bitcoin for you and you log in with an account. It's simple, but you depend on the company and on keeping your account secure.

Self-custody means you hold the keys yourself, usually in a wallet. Nobody can freeze your coins, but nobody can help if you lose access. Mistakes are your responsibility.

You don't need a wallet on day one

If you're stacking €10 to €50 a month, moving coins out costs a lot in proportion. Withdrawing from Revolut X costs about £3 (roughly €3.50) plus the network fee (see the fee calculator), which is roughly 7% of a €50 balance. A hardware wallet also costs money.

A reasonable approach is to save until the amount feels meaningful to you, then withdraw rarely and in one go. What counts as meaningful is your call.

Seed phrase basics

  • A seed phrase (usually 12 or 24 words) is the master backup of your wallet. Anyone who has it can take your Bitcoin.
  • Write it on paper or metal. Don't photograph it or store it in cloud notes, email or a password manager.
  • Never type it into a website or share it, even with "support". Real support never asks for it.
  • If you lose both the device and the phrase, the Bitcoin is gone for good.
  • Keep a backup in a separate safe place, and test recovery with a small amount first.
