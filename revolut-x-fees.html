Withdrawing BTC to your own wallet

Revolut charges a service fee of £3 (or the local-currency equivalent, roughly €3.50) for Bitcoin, plus the blockchain network fee, per withdrawal. The app shows the exact total before you confirm.

Tip: withdraw rarely. Buy small amounts regularly, and withdraw when you've saved up enough that the fixed fee is a small share.

Figures come from each platform's own official fee page and can change. Last checked . Check current fees in the platform before you trade.

  • Revolut X: 0% maker / 0.09% taker, same at any volume (Revolut X fees).
  • Kraken Pro: entry tier (under $2,500 30-day volume) 0.40% maker / 0.80% taker (Kraken fee schedule).
  • Coinbase Advanced: entry tier for EU/UK 0.25% maker / 0.50% taker, per Coinbase's September 2026 announcement. The US entry tier is 0.50% / 0.90% (Coinbase announcement). Your region and tier may differ.
  • Revolut app (Standard/Plus plan, EEA): the greater of a minimum fee (€0.99–€2.99 for trades under €200) or 1.49%, plus a one-sided added spread that Revolut says typically ranges 0–3%. Paid plans pay less (Revolut crypto fees). Plans also have a monthly exchange limit above which an extra fee applies. Not modelled here.
